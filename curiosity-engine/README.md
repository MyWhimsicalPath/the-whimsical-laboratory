# Curiosity Engine 🧪

A one-hour-at-a-time learning system for developing creative agency: the ability to investigate something unfamiliar, understand enough of it, and turn an idea into something that works.

## Rules

- The hour is a container, not a score.
- There are no streaks and no catch-up.
- Experiments are numbered, not dated as days.
- Leave evidence: code, sketches, notes, prototypes, questions, or useful failures.
- Make → encounter a gap → learn → modify → make again.
- Curiosity is allowed to branch, but depth matters too.

## Season I — The Laboratory Awakens

**Primary territory:** Creative technology  
**Objective:** Move from “I can follow and modify things” toward “I can figure out how to make a small idea work.”
